The Navigation Map#

The BPT Navigation Map is the conceptual blueprint of your good EA Navigator. Navigating the whole organization,

The Three Segments#

  1. B (Business): The Captain’s decision layer. Owners of the investment.
  2. P (Product): The Business Analyst layer. Owners of the functional logic.
  3. T (Technology): The Implementation layer. Navigating around Legacy Icebergs. Deploying the products.

The Stake Holders View#

The Organization Navigator (Enterprise Architect) uses this as a map to ensure that the “Technology Pistons” are capable of delivering the “Business Intent” without hitting hidden risks. And forever de risking the Depths of the Technical Debt.
