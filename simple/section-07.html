The Mesh: DBJ Taxonomy#

DBJ Taxonomy is the common language. It names the parts of the organization’s information space, and everyone uses the same names.

Two levels. Four Categories, each holding four Capabilities.

CategoryHolds
ConceptualBusiness, Information, Application, Technology
LogicalData Management, Integration, Platform, Security
PhysicalCompute, Infrastructure, Network, Storage
ImplementationDeployment, Development, Monitoring, Operations

It does three jobs: it locates a concern, it gives roles a shared vocabulary, and it makes knowledge findable twice.

No role renames or reinterprets these. That is the whole point of having them. {: .note}

Taxonomy comes before the climb — you cannot measure maturity in terms nobody agrees on.
