Operating Model — Business · Product · Technology

DBJ Method



DBJ Method encapsulates Enterprise Architecture artifacts to guide the dynamic, smooth transition and running of the business, towards the full AI enablement.

First half is actually preparing the team to play and win in the premiership league.

Second half of the DBJ Method is premiership league playground; it is so called “BPT” operating model; loop made of three domains: Business, Products and Technology. Business declares the Product, and Technology is implementing the Product. Product domain balances them all.

The founding principle of the DBJ Method is to encapsulate the TOGAF artifacts into the plain sailing operation. To extend the sailing analogy: DBJ Method is the optimal path, for navigating the business.

Method Adoption

Without anyone noticing, DBJ Method is repeatedly raising the AI feasibility level in adopters organization. That is the core narrow focus.

No point in hiding the key surprise: in some organizations, DBJ Method adoption does require fundamental change. But no part of it is revolutionary or sensational. So, large portion of adopters will have simpler time onboarding. It is just formalized mature operating practices. Already followed by most.

Two parts staged on-boarding

Depending on the adopters organization size and complexity, the initial team is selected and formed. In some cases the whole operational part of the organization can undertake the DBJ Method Onboarding. It is important for at least one person from the Business, Product and Technology to form the nucleus.

In the first step, qualified DBJ Method trainers, prepare clients roles for architecture-led delivery by gently putting them on firm (DBJ simplified) capability maturity foundations.

Team Capabilities and Maturity

DBJ Method Practitioners are leading their client’s current organizational maturity to the (DBJ) ACMM Levels L0–L5.

During this period the team, importantly:

  • Establishes a common vocabulary: following the DBJ Taxonomy
    • Future shared language of the organization’s information space
  • Classifies organization and team capability gaps
  • Sets a realistic initial target level (L3 to L5)

Business, Product, Technology Operations Loop

Once on-boarded to maturity Level 3 (or above), the client organization enters the BPT training. A continuous cycle of three clearly decoupled segments: Business, Product and Technology, connected by constant flow of organization events.

BPT domains are communicating by exchanging messages and deliverables from their projects based on DBJ ADM “Wheel”.


Some of the benefits for BPT adopters

  • Decoupling of responsibilities on the organization level results in fast but resilient operations
  • Product becomes the natural alignment point between Business and Technology
  • Enterprise Architecture viewpoint is improved by operating “above”, not within
  • CMM level 3+ onboarding prerequisite provides organization competitiveness
  • Continuous loop aligns to operational rhythm, not rigid phase gates
  • DBJ ADM “Wheel’s” and BPT segments depend on each other but they turn decoupled of each other.

DBJ Method adoption, provides to the customer organization, the bridge over which users are crossing from the chaos of Legacy to the feasibility of AI. Avoiding the chaos of AI.
