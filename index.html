    Smooth business sailing, riding the AI enabled operating model



    • First half is actually preparing the team to play and win in the premiership league.
    • Second half of the DBJ Method is premiership league playground
      • It is assembled from the so called “BPT” operating model
      • loop made of three domains: Business, Products and Technology.
      • Business declares the Product, and Technology is implementing the Product. Product domain balances them all.

    The founding principle was to encapsulate the TOGAF architecture artifacts, for the plain sailing business operation.

    To extend the sailing analogy: DBJ Method is the business navigation instrument.

    Adoption

    Without anyone noticing, DBJ Method is repeatedly raising the AI feasibility level in adopters organizations. That is the core and narrow focus.

    No point in hiding the key surprise: in some organizations, DBJ Method adoption does require fundamental change, up-front. But no part of it is revolutionary or sensational. Large portion of adopters will have simpler time onboarding. DBJ Methods is just mature operating practices, formalized. Already followed by most.

    On-boarding

    Depending on the adopters organization size and complexity, the initial team is formed. In some cases the whole operational part of the organization can undertake the DBJ Method Onboarding. It is important for at least one person from the Business, Product and Technology to form the nucleus, for the future guidance.

    Qualified DBJ Method trainers, prepare clients roles for architecture-led delivery by gently putting them on firm (DBJ simplified) capability maturity foundations.

    Team Capabilities and Maturity

    DBJ Method Practitioners are leading their client’s current organizational maturity.

    During this period the team, importantly:

    • Establishes a common vocabulary
    • Future shared language of the organization’s information space
    • Classifies organization and team capability gaps
    • Sets realistic initial targets

    Do not deliver the wrong thing fast

    Securely Ride the BPT Loop

    Once on-boarded to maturity initial level 3 (or above), the client organization enters the BPT training. A continuous cycle of three clearly decoupled segments: Business, Product and Technology, connected by constant flow of organization events.

    BPT domains are communicating by exchanging messages and deliverables from their projects.

    DBJ Method adoption, provides to the customer organization, the bridge over which users are crossing from the chaos of Legacy to the feasibility of AI.
