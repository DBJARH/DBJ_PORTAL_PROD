The bridge, enabling the safe journey from the chaos of legacy to an AI-enabled future.


DBJ Method encapsulates Enterprise Architecture artifacts to guide the dynamic, smooth running of the business, to wards the full AI enablement.

Second half of the DBJ Method, “BPT” is a operating model loop made of three domains: Business, Products and Technology. Business declares the Product, and Technology is implementing the Product.

The principle of the DBJ Method is to encapsulate the TOGAF artifacts into the plain sailing operation. To extend the sailing analogy: The DBJ Method is the optimal path, for navigating the feasible business.

On-boarding

In the first step DBJ Method trainers, prepare clients for architecture-led delivery by gently putting them on firm (DBJ simplified) capability maturity foundations.

The Maturity Levels

DBJ Method Practitioners are leading their client’s current organizational maturity to the (DBJ) ACMM Levels L0–L5. In this step DBJ Method Practitioners:

During that period organization

  • Establishes a common vocabulary: following the DBJ Taxonomy — to be the shared language of the organization’s information space
  • Classifies capability gaps using the ACMM scorecard
  • Set a realistic initial target level (L3)

Business, Product, Technology Operations Loop

Once on-boarded to maturity Level 3 (or above), the client organization enters the BPT — a continuous cycle of three clearly decoupled segments: Business, Product and Technology, connected by constant flow of organization events.

BPT domains are communicating by exchanging deliverables from their projects based on DBJ ADM “Wheel”.

Some of benefits for BPT adopters

  • Decoupling of responsibilities on the organization level results in fast but resilient development
  • Product becomes the natural alignment point between Business and Technology
  • Enterprise Architecture guides by operating “above”, not within
  • CMM level 3+ prerequisite provides foundational maturity
  • Continuous loop maps to operational rhythm, not rigid phase gates
  • DBJ ADM “Wheel’s” and BPT segments depend on each other but they operate decoupled of each other.

DBJ Method adoption, provides to the customer organization, the bridge over which users of the method cross from the chaos of Legacy to the feasibility of AI.
